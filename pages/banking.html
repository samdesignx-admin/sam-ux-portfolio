Digital bankingClarity at high-stakes moments

Account opening and omnichannel banking shaped around confidence, transparency and recovery.

Make an enterprise journey feel understandable without oversimplifying it.

Online account opening and banking journeys ask people to make financial choices, share sensitive information and recover from errors while maintaining trust. I led digital-transformation work across these experiences, connecting UX strategy, interaction design, behavioral data, personalization and scalable UI patterns.

Leadership starts with the decision system

Rather than treating the work as screen design, I focused the team on friction points across the journey and made the trade-offs visible to stakeholders. We connected behavioral signals and research insights to clearer information architecture, progressive disclosure and reusable interaction patterns—so user needs, risk and delivery realities could be evaluated together.

Design system as an operating tool

The modular system supported enterprise applications and multiple digital channels. Its value was not visual consistency alone: it created a common interaction language, helped teams make decisions with shared patterns, and reduced avoidable design-to-development interpretation as the product evolved.

Good product design is the work of making complexity understandable—especially when confidence is part of the product.

Outcome and leadership impact

The supplied portfolio context records improvements in account-opening time, conversion, task completion and feature adoption, without publishing the redacted percentages. More importantly, the work positioned design as a partner in business metrics, research and cross-functional decision-making rather than a final UI layer.
