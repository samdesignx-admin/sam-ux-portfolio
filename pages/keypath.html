KeyPath

A guided product-experience exploration for a purchase journey that requires many decisions before a person feels ready to act.

Home-buying journeyGive the next decision a clear path.

A product system organized around orientation, choices and momentum.

Turn a complex purchase into a journey people can navigate with confidence.

Home buying asks people to connect goals, information and time-sensitive decisions across a long, unfamiliar process. KeyPath positions the experience as a guided roadmap rather than a loose collection of listings or tasks.

Strategy: orient first, then reveal the right next step

The product-design focus is information hierarchy: establish where a person is in the journey, make the immediate decision understandable, and reveal complexity when it becomes relevant. That creates a useful balance between guidance and user control.

From page to interaction system

The project includes a dedicated web experience with separate structure, CSS and JavaScript. In portfolio terms, it represents how I translate a journey model into an interaction and visual system while keeping claims about business outcomes appropriately bounded.

Leadership lens

KeyPath shows my approach to product ambiguity: frame the journey, align the information model to the user’s decision, and make a complex service feel more navigable.
