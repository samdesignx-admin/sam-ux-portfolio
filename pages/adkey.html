The problem

Advertising attention is fragmented across channels. A person may see an ad on a billboard, TV, print piece or social post, but the experience often ends at the moment of exposure. ADKey creates a persistent identifier that can travel with the advertisement and connect that moment of attention to a richer digital experience.

Product strategy

The core design decision was to make the interaction model simple enough to work across channels: create an advertisement, assign a unique AdKey, generate a dynamic QR code, distribute it anywhere, and measure what happens next.

One interaction model across every channel

  • Create a rich digital ad experience.
  • Generate a persistent AdKey and dynamic QR code.
  • Place the same key across print, OOH, TV, social and physical environments.
  • Give audiences a consistent way to continue the experience.
  • Measure scans, views, clicks and downstream actions.
The strategy is to make an advertisement discoverable, persistent and measurable after the original impression.

Designing the product system

The UX spans both sides of the ecosystem: an advertiser workflow for creating and managing campaigns, and a public experience for entering or scanning an AdKey. The visual direction uses a high-contrast black and yellow system so the product feels direct, recognizable and infrastructure-oriented.

Why it belongs in my portfolio

ADKey demonstrates product thinking beyond a single interface: defining a new interaction primitive, designing an end-to-end ecosystem, and connecting physical touchpoints, digital destinations and analytics into one experience.
