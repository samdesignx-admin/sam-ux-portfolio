One recognizable entry point for a richer, measurable ad experience.

Turn a fragmented impression into a coherent product journey.

Advertising attention is spread across print, out-of-home, TV, social and physical environments. ADKey frames that fragmentation as a product problem: the audience needs a simple way to continue, while the advertiser needs a shared system for creating and managing the experience.

Strategy: reduce the interaction to one repeatable action

The core decision is a persistent AdKey paired with a dynamic QR code. An advertiser creates an experience once, assigns it a key, and can place that same entry point wherever the campaign appears. For the audience, the interaction is legible: scan or enter the key, then continue in the right context.

Create & publish

A campaign workflow for configuring a destination and generating a reusable key.

Continue the experience

A public path that works whether someone encounters the campaign digitally or in the world.

Learn from engagement

A shared view of scans, views, clicks and downstream actions.

Designing both sides of the ecosystem

The concept treats the advertiser workflow and the public experience as one system rather than two isolated screens. The high-contrast visual language helps the key read as infrastructure: direct, recognizable and designed to travel across channels.

Leadership lens

This work shows how I define a new interaction model, align an ecosystem around it, and use information architecture to connect a brand touchpoint to a measurable product journey.
