Prayer Portal

A digital community experience designed around a sensitive human need: giving people a clear way to ask for support, participate meaningfully and return without turning care into another engagement loop.

A calm structure for private intent and visible acts of support.

Design participation without turning care into a feed.

A prayer community has a different emotional contract from a typical social product. People may arrive with a private concern, need to understand how their request will be handled, or want to support someone else without performing that support publicly.

The challenge is therefore not simply navigation. It is creating an interaction model that communicates safety, purpose and respect while still making participation easy.

My role: define the experience around trust

I framed the experience around the core journeys rather than around a list of features: create or discover a request, understand its state, offer support, and return to the community. This keeps the product model focused and gives every interface element a clear reason to exist.

Strategy: make purpose and privacy legible

The design uses a restrained hierarchy and small set of meaningful actions. Content and participation cues are explicit so people understand what happens after they submit a request and what their participation means.

Ask

A clear entry point for sharing a request with enough context and control.

Support

A respectful interaction model for responding without creating social pressure.

Return

Simple states and cues that help members reconnect with requests and community activity.

Service design beyond the screen

The interface is one touchpoint in a larger service. Content states, notification moments and participation expectations need to work together so members understand the community's norms. Thinking across those touchpoints is what keeps the experience coherent as the product grows.

Leadership impact

This concept demonstrates how I translate an ambiguous human need into a service structure: define the journeys, establish trust boundaries, align content and interaction patterns, and create an experience where the technology stays in service of the community.
