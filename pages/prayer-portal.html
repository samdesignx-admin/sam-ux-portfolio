Community UX · service design · information architecture

What I would lead with

The repository was provided as one of your portfolio projects. I am positioning it as a service-design and community UX case study, while avoiding unsupported implementation details until the repository contents are accessible.

Design strategy

The case study is framed around the decisions behind the experience: who the product serves, what the primary journey is, how information is structured, where AI or automation adds value, how the interface communicates state, and how the design can scale beyond the first release.

Interaction principles

  • Make the primary task obvious.
  • Use progressive disclosure for complexity.
  • Make system state visible and recoverable.
  • Keep AI assistance explainable and controllable.
  • Design reusable patterns before adding visual polish.
The portfolio story should show not only what I built, but how I decide what should be built.

Portfolio outcome

This project strengthens the broader story of my portfolio: strategy-led product design, emerging technology, interaction systems and the ability to move from concept to working experience.
