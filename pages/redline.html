Redline

A working AI product that turns UX evaluation into a repeatable, structured workflow.

UX auditEvidence before opinion.

Structured evaluation for heuristics, accessibility, trust and cognitive load.

AI assistance designed around a UX decision workflow.

Redline accepts screenshots, PDFs or a URL and produces a structured UX audit covering Nielsen heuristics, WCAG, trust, conversion and cognitive load. It can also generate a 12-slide presentation deck.

Design strategy

The product focuses on who it serves, how information is structured, where automation adds value, and how the interface communicates state. Reusable patterns and explainable assistance support a workflow that can extend beyond a first release.

Portfolio outcome

A practical example of strategy-led AI product design, interaction systems and the ability to move from concept to working experience.
