UXNest — Nest Audit

Designing an AI-powered UX audit workflow that turns raw screens into structured, senior-level design feedback.

From “AI analysis” to a structured design decision system.

The product problem

UXNest is designed to let a user upload screenshots/PDFs or enter a website URL and receive a structured UX audit. The audit evaluates Nielsen heuristics, WCAG, trust, conversion and cognitive load, and is presented as feedback from a senior UX design director.

Experience strategy

The product is organized around a useful progression: provide evidence → analyze → prioritize → communicate. The report is not just an AI response; it is intended to become a decision artifact that helps a team understand what to fix and why.

The key design move is turning AI output into actionable UX evidence rather than an opaque paragraph of recommendations.

Product architecture

The repository describes a React + Vite frontend, a Vercel serverless proxy for the Anthropic API, account and history flows, audit quotas, analytics, email verification and an optional Supabase/Postgres architecture for moving account, audit and quota data server-side.

Trust & responsible AI

The product explicitly identifies limitations instead of hiding them. Google sign-in is designed to become server-verified; browser-side account and quota storage is documented as a prototype limitation; and the support assistant is grounded in a fixed knowledge base so it does not invent features or policies.

Report design

Each completed audit can produce a 12-slide presentation deck. The report brief can also be passed to an assistant so users can ask questions such as what to fix first or why an accessibility score is low, with answers grounded in the audit findings.

Why this matters as a portfolio piece

This is a strong demonstration of product strategy, AI interaction design, UX evaluation, information architecture, trust design and pragmatic product engineering — not simply an AI demo.
