UXNest

An AI-assisted UX-audit workflow that turns screens, PDFs or a URL into structured findings a team can use to decide what to address next.

Evidence → analysis → prioritiesTurn an audit into a decision artifact.

Structured around Nielsen heuristics, WCAG, trust, conversion and cognitive load.

AI output is only useful when a team can understand, prioritize and act on it.

UXNest accepts screenshots, PDFs or a website URL and produces a structured audit. The product problem is not simply generating critique; it is translating evidence into a sequence that supports design and product decisions.

Experience strategy

The workflow follows a deliberate progression: provide evidence, analyze, prioritize and communicate. Findings are designed to become a report a team can discuss—not an opaque paragraph of recommendations. Completed audits can generate a 12-slide presentation deck, extending the product into stakeholder alignment.

Responsible AI is part of the interaction

The repository documents prototype constraints directly, including browser-side account and quota storage and planned server verification for Google sign-in. The support assistant is grounded in a fixed knowledge base, making limitations and product boundaries visible instead of implying certainty.

Leadership lens

UXNest demonstrates end-to-end product thinking: defining the primary job, structuring complex information, designing for trust, and connecting AI capability to a human decision-making workflow.
