A compact hierarchy for attention-constrained interaction.

Prioritize what deserves immediate visibility.

Wearables are personal, contextual and attention-constrained. This exploration uses personas and journey thinking to identify the moments where a device can genuinely reduce friction.

Research-to-design thinking

Personas frame intent; journey mapping identifies high-value moments; UI concepts turn those moments into concise interactions. This sequence avoids treating a small screen as simply a smaller phone.

Interface strategy

Large targets, recognizable states, compact hierarchy and fast recovery support interaction at a glance. The design aims to make the next action clear before attention moves elsewhere.

Leadership lens

A compact case study in translating context into a product system—using user understanding to decide what belongs in the interface and what should stay out.
