Design intelligenceAccelerate thinking, not judgment.

AI assistance designed to keep evidence, state and user control visible.

Make AI useful inside the design process—not adjacent to it.

This concept explores an AI-assisted UX workflow. Its portfolio value is in the product questions it raises: what information the designer provides, where automation can reduce repetitive work, and how the system communicates confidence and limitation.

Strategy: make assistance inspectable

The experience is framed around a primary task, progressive disclosure for complexity and clear system state. Instead of presenting an answer as authority, the design makes space for a designer to review, redirect and use the output as input to a better decision.

Interaction principles

  • Make the primary task obvious.
  • Keep AI assistance explainable and controllable.
  • Use reusable patterns before adding visual polish.
  • Design recovery paths when the output is incomplete or wrong.

Leadership lens

A concise exploration of how I approach emerging technology: start with the human workflow, define the decision boundaries, and design an experience that earns trust through clarity.
