01 / Case study

Digital Account Opening

A banking transformation focused on replacing branch-heavy account opening with an omnichannel, digital-first journey.

RoleUI/UX Design Lead · Team of 7
ModelAgile
UX duration3 months
Timeline1 year 6 months

Problem → Situation

The portfolio identifies four core problems: no digital presence for account opening, complex form filling in the branch, weaker customer relationship management, and an account-opening/funding process taking at least a week.

Objectives → Tasks

The strategy targeted an omnichannel, frictionless experience; reduced form filling; an in-house CRM; account opening in four minutes or less; real-time funding; reusable design patterns; and a stated first-year deposit goal of $1.2 billion.

Research

Understand

Contextual inquiry, user interviews, web analytics and competitive analysis informed personas, journey maps and the opportunity space.

UX strategy

Define & ideate

Customer needs, business goals, success measures, constraints and available resources were brought into one decision framework.

Design

Build & test

Wireframes, clickable prototypes, high-fidelity UI, reusable patterns and iterative testing translated strategy into a deliverable system.

Research artifacts

The original work includes contextual inquiry, user interview materials, personas, an emotional journey map, analytical data and a competitive-analysis report. Security-sensitive names and photographs were changed in the portfolio.

What changed

The portfolio records branch tablet adoption, instant verification and funding, an internal CRM and reusable design patterns. It also reports that the account-opening process could be completed in less than four minutes by an efficient user.

50%Revenue generated in the 1st year versus the stated $1.2B goal
2×Revenue generated in the 2nd year
<4 minReported account-opening time for an efficient user
02 / Case study

Redesigning BankofHope.com

A mobile-first website strategy connecting digital banking tools, persona-based experiences, marketing content and reusable systems.

RoleUI/UX Lead · Team of 10
ModelAgile
UX duration3 months
Timeline1 year 6 months

Problem → Situation

The original case study identifies missing pointers to internally built digital tools, complex product-selection decisions, the need to increase website traffic, and an outdated visual experience.

Strategy → Tasks

The redesign focused on a mobile-first modern UI, persona-based homepages, upsell and cross-sell opportunities, optimized marketing content and a financial calculator.

Experience

Persona-led

Shape landing and home experiences around customer context instead of presenting every visitor with the same starting point.

Growth

Measure

Connect content, social promotion, calculators and calls to action to measurable customer behavior.

Scale

Operate

Build CMS capabilities, reusable patterns and a content approach that teams can maintain beyond the initial redesign.

Outcome

The portfolio reports mobile traffic increasing from 55% to 80%, stronger trust and online activity from existing customers, increased customer acquisition through social channels, and a CMS for backend content management.

80%Mobile traffic, up from 55%
25×Reported traction to the new website versus the old site
2×Revenue generated in the 2nd year versus the stated goal comparison
