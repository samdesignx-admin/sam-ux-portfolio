A spatial-computing exploration for connecting property information to the physical neighborhood where decisions are made.

Spatial discoveryContext first. Detail when invited.

Property information layered onto the world without overwhelming it.

Reduce the distance between a listing and the place it represents.

Real-estate discovery asks users to mentally connect listings, maps, photos and a physical neighborhood. This concept explores how AR could bring relevant context forward while keeping the environment—not the interface—primary.

Strategy: layer information by intent

Glanceable cues support discovery; deeper details appear when the person chooses to investigate. That progression keeps spatial interaction useful rather than decorative and makes the transition from exploration to property detail explicit.

Production questions matter early

A real spatial experience would need clear treatment of privacy, accuracy, accessibility, motion, battery use and the difference between verified property facts and contextual estimates. Naming those constraints is part of responsible product strategy.

Leadership lens

This is an exploration of emerging interaction models through a product-design lens: identify the user’s context, define what information earns attention, and make trust visible in the experience.
