Task prioritizationCapture quickly. See what matters.

A concise system for timing, priority and completion.

Keep a reminder product from becoming another source of clutter.

As tasks accumulate, the product must help people capture an intention, scan what matters and complete it without unnecessary navigation or decision fatigue.

Strategy: make priority visible

The concept centers quick capture, strong hierarchy and lightweight interactions. It emphasizes the next relevant action rather than presenting every task with equal visual weight.

Interaction and UI direction

Structured cards, compact controls and explicit states make the experience scannable and action-oriented. The system treats timing and completion as core information, not secondary settings.

Leadership lens

A focused mobile exploration showing how I use hierarchy and interaction design to turn an everyday utility into a clearer decision environment.
