Design philosophy

My UX is a
decision system.

Strong UX is not a sequence of attractive screens. It is a connected set of decisions about users, business outcomes, technology, behavior and constraints.

01

Frame the real problem

Start with user goals, business constraints and the underlying workflow. Avoid jumping directly into UI.

02

Design the system

Find reusable patterns, information structures and interaction models that can scale beyond one feature.

03

Prove the direction

Use research, behavioral signals, usability and stakeholder feedback to reduce uncertainty and improve decisions.
