Design philosophy

My UX is a decision system.

Strong UX is not a sequence of attractive screens. It is a connected set of decisions about users, business outcomes, technology, behavior and constraints.

01

Frame the real problem

Start with goals, constraints and the underlying workflow before jumping into UI.

02

Design the system

Find reusable patterns, information structures and interaction models that scale.

03

Prove the direction

Use research, behavioral signals, usability and stakeholder feedback to reduce uncertainty.
