02 / Leadership approach

Align the why.
Design the way.

My leadership starts before the interface. I clarify the problem, expose assumptions, connect user needs to business outcomes, and create a shared decision model. From there, I guide teams toward experiences and systems they can actually deliver and evolve.

01

Frame

Turn ambiguity into a clear problem space, identify the highest-value questions and give teams a reasoned direction.

02

Align

Bring research, product strategy, stakeholder goals, technical constraints and customer needs into the same conversation.

03

Lead

Set experience principles, mentor designers, facilitate difficult decisions and create the conditions for teams to do their best work.

04

Scale

Build reusable patterns, systems and ways of working that turn individual design decisions into consistent product quality.
